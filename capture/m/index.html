Put Capture on your Home Screen. It opens straight to New Lead, even with no signal.

Add Capture to Your Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (or the top of Chrome).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Capture from your Home Screen. It asks you to scan a code on your computer.

Install Capture

Tap Install. Capture gets its own icon and opens like an app.

  1. Open the browser menu ⋮ and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. Open Capture from your Home Screen. It asks you to scan a code on your computer.

Setting up from Capture on your computer? Keep that screen open. The next step is there.

Scan the Code on Your Computer

On your computer, open Capture and choose Set Up My Phone. Point this phone at the code.

Or Type the Code

The 6 characters under the code on your computer.

This phone works only for capturing your own leads. It never signs in to PULSE Hub.
