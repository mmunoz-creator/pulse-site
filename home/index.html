    Three things to know

      Indicators

      Each tile opens the series, its source and release cadence.

      Client cut parameters

      Client edition PDF export

      TemporaryExport the client edition as a PDF

      Shows an Export PDF button to Hub Admins on the client cut (the Preview client cut toggle and View as client). Meant for sharing the client edition with clients and prospects until they are on PULSE. Turn it off once client users are live; the button disappears at once for everyone. Each export goes in the Activity log with the month and the person's name.

      The switch saves with Save parameters above. The PDF is the client cut as this page shows it, printed as a newsletter: the sections listed for the client cut, what it means for building owners, the data vintage and the disclaimer. Nothing marked internal is in it.

      Executive readers

      People outside the Hub who get the internal edition as a two-page PDF by email, on the 11th at 8 a.m. Eastern. No Hub account is made. Every page and email says "Confidential, for internal users only." The PULSE lens is never in it.
